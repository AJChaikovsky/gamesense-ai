## Stamina management
Don't drain the stamina bar to zero. An empty bar means no dodge and broken guards. Release block between hits so stamina regenerates.

## Dodging
Roll toward or diagonally past the boss rather than straight away from it. Rolling backward often lands you in the end of a long reach attack.

## Spacing
Stay at a distance where you can punish but most attacks whiff. Many bosses have long gap closers. Backing far away invites them.

## Parrying and deflecting
Parries reward exact timing and are risky against unfamiliar bosses. In Sekiro, deflecting (blocking at the moment of impact) builds the enemy's posture, and a full posture bar opens a deathblow.

## Stance and posture breaks
Heavy and jump attacks build stance damage (Elden Ring). When a large enemy staggers, move to its front and use a critical attack.

## Lock-on and camera
Lock-on helps against single bosses in open arenas. Turn it off against huge bosses or near walls, where it makes the camera fight you.

## Multiple enemies
Pull enemies one at a time with ranged attacks, and fight in open space or narrow doorways so you are never surrounded.
