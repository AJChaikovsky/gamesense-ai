## Upgrading
Weapon upgrades usually give more damage than character levels. Spend upgrade materials on the weapon you actually use.

## Banking currency
Spend runes or souls before risky areas or boss fights so you have less to lose.

## Ambushes
Enemies hide around corners, on ledges and above doorways. Approach new areas slowly with a shield or a dodge ready.

## Status effects
Poison and scarlet rot drain health over time and need a cure item. Bleed (hemorrhage) and frostbite build up, then trigger a burst. Back off when a buildup bar appears.

## Falling
Long drops are lethal. Look for a ledge path, or use a mount's double jump (Elden Ring) to break long falls.

## Stuck on an area
Explore elsewhere, level up, upgrade your weapon, or try a different weapon type. These games usually offer more than one path forward.
