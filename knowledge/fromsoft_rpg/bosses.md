## Learning patterns
Spend early attempts on watching and dodging, not damage. Learn which attacks have long recoveries and punish only those.

## When to heal
Heal after the boss finishes a combo and has a long recovery, or when it is far away. Many bosses punish healing on sight ("input reading"), so heal at distance or behind a pillar.

## Phase two
Bosses often change at around half health with new attacks or a grab. Have healing left and full health going into the phase change.

## Summons and spirit ashes
In Elden Ring, spirit ashes and cooperative summons split the boss's attention. Summon right at the start of the fight, near the summoning point.

## Running out of flasks
With no flasks left, play defensively and wait for safe openings. One trade too many ends the attempt.

## Before the boss door
Rest at the nearest site of grace or bonfire, check your flask split between health and FP, and use buffs right before entering.
