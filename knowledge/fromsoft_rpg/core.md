- Stamina drives everything: attacking, rolling and blocking all cost it. Keep some in reserve so you can always dodge.
- Punish only in the boss's recovery windows. One or two hits, then reset. Greed is the most common cause of death.
- Rolls have invincibility frames. Roll into or through attacks at the moment they land, not early.
- Heal when the enemy is recovering or you have distance, never right in front of an attack.
- Delayed attacks are common. Watch the weapon, not the wind-up animation.
- On death you drop your currency (runes or souls) at that spot. Recover it on the next attempt by reaching it without dying.
