## Post-plant as T
After the plant, play for time. Spread out to crossfire angles that cover the bomb and let CTs come to you. Don't chase kills. The bomb explodes after 40 seconds, and every second the CTs spend clearing helps you.

## Retakes and defusing as CT
Group up with teammates and retake together with utility: smoke off crossfires, flash before entering. Clear the site before you start a defuse. Without a kit the defuse takes 10 seconds, so tapping the bomb to bait is risky. With a kit it takes 5.

## Low health
With low HP, avoid taking first contact. Hold a passive angle, play for trades behind a teammate, or reposition. Anyone with a rifle can one-shot you.

## Trading
Stay close enough that if a teammate dies you can kill their killer within a second or two. Lurking far from the team means nobody can trade you.

## Rotations
Don't rotate on the first sound. Wait for information (several enemies, utility, the bomb) before leaving your site, and tell the team what you saw.

## After getting a kill
Don't re-peek the same angle immediately. Reposition or wait for a teammate, because the next enemy is pre-aiming where you just were.

## Clutches
In a 1vX, play time and information. Avoid fighting two enemies at once, and use the bomb (planting or defusing) to force them to come to you.
