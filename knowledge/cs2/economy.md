## Full buy
Buy a rifle, kevlar and helmet, and at least a smoke and a flash. As CT add a defuse kit when you can. If your money only covers a rifle with no armor, armor usually matters more than the upgrade from SMG to rifle.

## Eco and save rounds
When the team cannot full buy next round either way, save: buy nothing or just a pistol and kevlar, and play for exit frags or to keep weapons. Saving alone while the team buys loses rounds. Follow the team's call.

## Force buys
Force when the enemy is weak (after their eco or when they lost the pistol round), on the last round of a half, or when a loss bonus will still fund a full buy next round. SMGs, shotguns and armor are the efficient force buys.

## Loss bonus
Each consecutive round loss raises the bonus by $500, from $1,400 up to $3,400. Planting the bomb in a lost round gives the T side extra money. Losing streaks make full buys affordable again, so a well timed save often pays off.

## Last round of the half
Money resets to $800 at the half and at the start of each overtime half, so spend everything on the last round of a half. Saving gains nothing.

## Pistol rounds
Rounds 1 and 13 start at $800. Common buys are kevlar, a better pistol, or utility. Win the pistol round and the opponent is usually forced into an eco next round.

## Dropping weapons
If you have spare money and a teammate cannot afford a rifle, buy one and drop it. Ask for a drop when you are short.
