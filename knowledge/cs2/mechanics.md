## Crosshair placement
Keep the crosshair at head height on the angle an enemy is most likely to appear, before you see them. Small adjustments beat big flicks.

## Counter-strafing
Weapons are inaccurate while moving. Tap the opposite movement key to stop instantly before you shoot. Shooting while running is the most common reason for missed shots.

## Spray control
Rifles kick up then sideways. Burst at long range, spray only at close range, and pull down to compensate. Reset with a short pause between bursts.

## Peeking
Wide swing to catch an enemy holding a tight angle off guard. Jiggle or shoulder peek to get information without committing. Never peek an AWP the same way twice.

## Utility basics
Smokes block vision for about 20 seconds and are put out by HE grenades. Flash so the enemy is blinded when you peek, not before. Molotovs and incendiaries clear corners and delay pushes.

## Reloading
Reload behind cover, not in the open after a kill. If you have most of a magazine left in an active fight, switch to your pistol instead of reloading.
