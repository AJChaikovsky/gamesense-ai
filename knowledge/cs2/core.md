- Matches are MR12: sides swap after 12 rounds, first to 13 wins. Money resets to $800 at the half.
- Economy: buy together as a team. A full buy (rifle, armor, utility) costs about $4,000 to $5,500. Below that, save or force as a team, not alone.
- Loss bonus starts at $1,400 and grows $500 per consecutive loss up to $3,400.
- Kevlar is $650, kevlar plus helmet $1,000, a defuse kit $400 (CT only).
- The bomb explodes 40 s after the plant. A defuse takes 10 s, or 5 s with a kit.
- Fundamentals that win rounds: crosshair at head height on the next angle, stop moving before you shoot, trade your teammates, use utility before you peek, and don't re-peek the same angle after getting a kill.
