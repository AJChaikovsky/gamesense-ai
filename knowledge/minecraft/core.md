- The first day: punch trees, make a crafting table, wooden then stone tools, gather food, and build or dig a shelter before night.
- Night spawns hostile mobs in the dark. Sleeping in a bed skips the night and sets your spawn point. Beds explode in the Nether and End.
- Hunger: health regenerates only when the hunger bar is nearly full. With 3 or fewer drumsticks you can't sprint.
- Light up areas with torches. Mobs spawn in darkness, including in caves and around your base.
- Creepers explode near you. Back away or hit them and retreat. Zombies and skeletons burn in daylight but creepers don't.
- Always carry food, a weapon and blocks. A water bucket prevents fall damage.
- HUD: hearts (health) sit above the hotbar on the left, drumsticks (hunger) on the right, armor above the hearts, and air bubbles above the hunger bar while underwater. Empty armor slots mean no armor.
- Underwater, the air bubbles drain and then you take drowning damage: swim up for air. Respiration and Aqua Affinity help later.
- First wood: turn logs into planks, then four planks into a crafting table. Wooden tools break quickly, so upgrade to stone tools from cobblestone soon.
- Food: pigs, cows, sheep and chickens drop meat. Cook it in a furnace or campfire, since raw meat restores little. Rotten flesh gives the Hunger effect, so eat it only in an emergency.
- Without armor, avoid zombies and other hostile mobs, especially at night. Back away or get behind blocks rather than fighting.
- Villages have beds, food and villagers who trade useful items for emeralds. Workstations show a villager's job, e.g. a stonecutter (mason) cuts stone into stairs, slabs and walls with no waste.
- Sweet berry bushes slow you and deal damage when you walk through them. Pick the berries instead of walking through.
- Wolves are neutral: feed them bones to tame them as companions. Don't hit them, or the pack attacks.
- Thunderstorms let mobs spawn even in daytime, and lightning can strike. Shelter or sleep through them.
