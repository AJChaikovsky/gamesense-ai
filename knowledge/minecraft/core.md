- The first day: punch trees, make a crafting table, wooden then stone tools, gather food, and build or dig a shelter before night.
- Night spawns hostile mobs in the dark. Sleeping in a bed skips the night and sets your spawn point. Beds explode in the Nether and End.
- Hunger: health regenerates only when the hunger bar is nearly full. With 3 or fewer drumsticks you can't sprint.
- Light up areas with torches. Mobs spawn in darkness, including in caves and around your base.
- Creepers explode near you. Back away or hit them and retreat. Zombies and skeletons burn in daylight but creepers don't.
- Always carry food, a weapon and blocks. A water bucket prevents fall damage.
