## Surviving the first night
Dig into a hillside or build a small hut, seal the entrance and light it with torches. Spend the night crafting or mining nearby rather than wandering outside.

## Food
Cooked meat restores far more hunger than raw. Kill animals, cook in a furnace, and start a wheat farm and an animal pen early. Golden carrots and steak are the best common foods.

## Creepers
Creepers hiss and then explode, destroying blocks. Sprint-hit them to knock them back, then retreat. A shield blocks most of the damage.

## Skeletons
Skeletons shoot from range. Close in using cover, or block arrows with a shield. Fight them in daylight, when they burn.

## Endermen
Don't look at an enderman's face. If one is angry, stand under a two-block-high ceiling or in water, where it can't follow.

## Phantoms
Phantoms appear after you go three in-game days without sleeping. Sleep regularly to prevent them.

## Low health
Retreat, block yourself in with a few blocks, and eat to fill the hunger bar so health regenerates. Don't fight at low health without armor.

## Spiders and cave spiders
Spiders climb walls and are hostile at night. Cave spiders in mineshafts poison you. Break their spawners with torches placed around them.
