## Tool progression
Wood, then stone, then iron, then diamond, then netherite. Iron needs a stone pickaxe, diamond needs iron, obsidian needs diamond. Make iron armor as soon as you have about 24 iron ingots.

## Finding diamonds
In 1.18 and later, diamonds are most common deep down, around Y -58. Strip mine or explore deep caves, and bring torches, food and a water bucket.

## Lava safety
Lava is common at deep levels. Never dig straight down, never dig straight up into unknown blocks, and keep a water bucket on your hotbar to put out lava and yourself.

## Inventory and hotbar
Keep a weapon, a pickaxe, food, torches and building blocks on the hotbar. Store everything else in chests at your base.

## Smelting
Furnaces smelt ore into ingots and cook food. Coal or charcoal is the usual fuel. A blast furnace smelts ore twice as fast.

## Enchanting
Build an enchanting table (obsidian, diamonds, a book) and surround it with bookshelves for stronger enchantments. Mending and Unbreaking keep your best tools alive.

## Going to the Nether
Build a portal from at least 10 obsidian and light it with flint and steel. Wear at least one piece of gold armor so piglins don't attack, bring fire resistance if you can, and mark your way back to the portal.
